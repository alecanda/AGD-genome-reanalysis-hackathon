# Challenge 1 — Talos Case Review

**Question:** What would make the Talos report genuinely useful during a clinical case review?

Start from the real Talos HTML output/code.

Pick 2–3 improvements and make them good rather than attempting a shallow redesign of everything.

Possible directions:
- case overview
- phenotype summary
- candidate table
- evidence panels
- filters
- reason explanations
- visualisation
- export

**Success:** a clinician can immediately explain why your redesigned view is better for reviewing a case.
