# Hackathon judging rubric — 100 points

| Criterion | Points |
|---|---:|
| Clinical usefulness | 30 |
| Novelty / creativity | 30 |
| Working prototype / demo | 20 |
| Potential value for Talos / reanalysis | 10 |
| Effective use of AI-assisted development | 10 |

## Guidance

### Clinical usefulness (30)
Does this address a real pain point for diagnostic/reanalysis work? Would a clinician or analyst actually want it?

### Novelty / creativity (30)
Is the idea surprising, clever or substantially different from the existing Talos workflow?

### Working prototype (20)
Can the team demonstrate the core idea rather than only describe it?

### Talos/reanalysis value (10)
Could this plausibly become a useful contribution, extension or design idea for Talos?

### AI-assisted development (10)
Did the team use the agent to accelerate work they otherwise would not have attempted or completed? This is about development acceleration, not about forcing an LLM into the product.

## Important

Technical complexity is not itself a goal. A simple, clinically valuable prototype can beat a sophisticated but poorly motivated one.

Potential awards:
- Best Overall
- Most Clinically Useful
- Most Creative
- Best Demonstration of AI-Assisted Development
- Best Potential Talos Contribution
