# Track 5 — Open pitch

Bring your own idea, anchored to the same theme: **genome reanalysis, variant prioritisation, and rare disease diagnostics.**

**Process**
1. Pitch your idea in one sentence during group formation (17:00–17:10) — this is how your group forms around it, so have it ready before then.
2. You're not locked in — if the team discussion at the start of the hackathon surfaces a better-scoped version of the idea, evolve it. Judges care about what you actually build, not whether it matches your original pitch verbatim.
3. Same rubric as every other track: weighted most on clinical usefulness and novelty. "Open-ended" is not a novelty bonus by itself — the idea itself needs to earn that score.

**Data**
Feel free to use [`../shared_data/mock_talos_candidates.json`](../shared_data/README.md) or Track 4's `mock_reanalysis_queue.csv` if they fit, synthesise your own mock data, or pull in genuinely public reference data (ClinVar, gnomAD, PanelApp, OMIM, GIAB) — just no real patient data.

**If you're short on inspiration**, a few prompts that didn't make the curated menu but are fair game here: SV consensus scoring across multiple callers, a local/offline PanelApp cache pattern, compound-het phasing confidence scoring, a cohort-level "recurrently hit gene" dashboard across multiple mock cases.
